Good food.

Sera means evening. The part of the day when the pace changes, another chair gets pulled up, and dinner becomes a little more than dinner.

Made for
sharing.

The kitchen

A concept built around Italian comfort: good bread, a bowl of pasta, a blistered pizza, and something sweet if you have room.

The table

Big plates in the middle. Forks reaching across. A first drink that turns into a whole evening. That is the atmosphere imagined here.

The details

Short menus. Clear prices. Ingredients you can understand. This site explores how a restaurant could feel online; it does not represent an operating restaurant.
